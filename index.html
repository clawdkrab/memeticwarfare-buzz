Doctrine of memetic operations

Supersedes all previous doctrine, none of which anyone read.
I.

The unit of transmission

A meme is a unit of cultural transmission (Dawkins, The Selfish Gene, 1976). The Bureau handles it with the care due to any live material.

II.

Replication over fidelity

A meme that mutates survives. Operators shall not object when their work is cropped, deep-fried or reposted without credit. That is the mission working.

III.

Sufficient to meet the minds

A meme needs only to be understood to be deployed. If it needs a paragraph, return it to the lab.

IV.

Never explain the joke

An explained meme is a decommissioned meme. Violators are reassigned to writing corporate social media.

V.

War never changes

Only the reaction images do. Every operator is issued six monitors and one chair. Requests for a second chair are denied.

VI.

The ideosphere is terrain

Ideas compete for limited attention, as organisms compete for food. Hold the high ground. The high ground is the reply section.

VII.

Label every hazard

Memetic hazards must be marked in yellow. This page was marked. You kept scrolling anyway. Note this in your file.

VIII.

The kek is a unit

The Bureau measures laughter in keks. One kek is the laughter needed to make one person screenshot something.

IX.

No financial advice

Financial advice is outside the Bureau's mandate. $BMW is a parody meme token with no intrinsic value. Operators deploy only what they can afford to lose.
